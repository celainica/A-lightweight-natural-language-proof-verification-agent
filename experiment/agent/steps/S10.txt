  proof: Let x in S and suppose g in Gamma_0 fixes x. Proper discontinuity implies the stabilizer (Gamma_0)_x is discrete, because for the compact set {x} only finitely many group elements can send it to intersect itself. Since the action is isometric, (Gamma_0)_x is a subgroup of the compact isotropy group Isom(S)_x, so (Gamma_0)_x is compact. A compact discrete group is finite. Therefore any point stabilizer in Gamma_0 is finite. As Gamma_0 is torsion-free, every finite subgroup is trivial. Hence g=1, and the action is free.
