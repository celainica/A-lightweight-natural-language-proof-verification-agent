  proof: Work with compactly supported singular cochains over Q. For a compactly supported cochain c on Y, pull back to p^*c on X. For a compactly supported cochain u on X, define the transfer tr(u) on a singular simplex sigma:Delta^m->Y by summing u over the two lifts of sigma to X:

  Because the cover has finite degree, only two lifts occur. If u is supported in a compact K subset X, then tr(u) is supported in the compact set p(K), so tr preserves compact support. At the cochain level,
  tr(p^*c)=2c
  and
  p^*(tr(u))=u+gamma^*u.
  Passing to cohomology and dividing by 2 over Q shows that p^* has inverse (1/2)tr on the invariant subspace. Hence p^*:H_c^*(Y;Q)->H_c^*(X;Q)^gamma is an isomorphism.
