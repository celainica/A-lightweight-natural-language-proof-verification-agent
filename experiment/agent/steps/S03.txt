The fundamental group Gamma acts on X by deck transformations. Because M is a manifold, this properly discontinuous action is strictly free. Therefore, the torsion element gamma acts on X as a free, finite-order homeomorphism. As a homeomorphism of X, gamma induces a linear pullback automorphism gamma^* on the compactly supported cohomology H_c^*(X;Q). Because H_c^n(X;Q) is a 1-dimensional Q-vector space and gamma^2=id, the induced map gamma^* must act as multiplication by +/-1. We evaluate the compactly supported Lefschetz number of gamma, defined algebraically as the alternating sum of the traces of the induced maps on cohomology. This collapses to a single non-vanishing term at k=n, yielding:

L_c(gamma,X)=sum_{k=0}^n (-1)^k Tr(gamma^*|H_c^k(X;Q))=(-1)^n(+/-1)!=0.
