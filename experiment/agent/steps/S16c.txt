A linear automorphism A extends to a continuous map \bar A:S^d->S^d fixing the point at infinity. Under the compactification isomorphism, the action of A on H_c^d(R^d;Q) is the action of \bar A on \widetilde H^d(S^d;Q). By polar decomposition, A is homotopic through invertible linear maps to an orthogonal matrix U, and the degree of the induced sphere map equals deg(U)=sign(det U)=sign(det A). Therefore A^* on H_c^d(R^d;Q) is multiplication by sign(det A). If A is an involution with +1-eigenspace dimension k, then its eigenvalues are +1 with multiplicity k and -1 with multiplicity d-k, so sign(det A)=(-1)^{d-k}.
